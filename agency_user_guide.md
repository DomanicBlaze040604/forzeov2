## The Agency Dashboard

When you first log in, or when no specific brand is selected, you are presented with the **Agency Dashboard**. This is your high-level control center for your entire client portfolio.

### Key Aggregate Metrics
- **Total Brands**: The number of active client brands you are currently managing.
- **Total Prompts**: The combined count of all search queries across your entire portfolio.
- **Avg Visibility**: Your global "Share of Voice" average across all clients and all AI models.

### Critical Alerts
The dashboard identifies **Brands Needing Attention** based on low visibility scores (below 30%). This allows you to prioritize your optimization efforts where they are needed most.

## Portfolio Management

Managing multiple clients is seamless with the Forzeo Brand Switcher and Portfolio Manager.

### Switching Between Brands
- Use the **Brand Selector** at the top of the sidebar.
- Switch directly to the **Agency Dashboard** for an overview.
- Select any client to open their dedicated optimization dashboard.

### Adding New Brands
1. Click **"Brands"** in the sidebar or **"Add Brand"** in the selector.
2. Enter the client's **Brand Name** and **Domain**.
3. Select the **Target Industry** and **Location**.
4. Use **AI Auto-Find** to instantly discover their top competitors.
5. Set a **Primary Color** to customize their dashboard experience.

## Running Audits & Campaigns

Forzeo tracks brand mentions across 6+ AI models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, etc.) in real-time.

### Individual Prompt Audits
Within a client dashboard, you can run single audits to check visibility for specific keywords. Use the **Discovery Engine** toggle to perform deep web analysis during the audit.

### Massive Topic Audits (Campaigns)
For comprehensive analysis, use the **Topics** tab:
1. Initialize a **New Topic Audit**.
2. Run all active prompts for a client as a single batch.
3. View aggregate SOV, average rank, and competitor leaderboards across the entire topic.

## Mastering Citation Intelligence

Understanding *where* AI models get their information is key to improving visibility.

### Citation Analysis
- **Analyze Citations**: Click to perform deep analysis on every source URL found in audit results.
- **Category Classification**: Sources are automatically grouped (UGC, Press, Competitor, etc.).
- **Opportunity Levels**:
    - 🟢 **Easy Wins**: Social media, own blog, Reddit threads.
    - 🟡 **Medium Effort**: Press releases, editorial mentions, app stores.
    - 🔴 **Difficult**: Highly regulated sources like Wikipedia.

## AI-Powered Insights & Signals

Leverage our advanced AI to do the heavy lifting for your clients.

### Strategic Recommendations
In the **Insights** tab, Forzeo generates a 3-tier action plan for every client:
- **Immediate (This Week)**: High-impact "quick wins."
- **Short-term (This Month)**: Content and link-building strategies.
- **Long-term (This Quarter)**: Major PR and authority-building goals.

### Fresh Signals
The **Future Citations** system monitors the web in real-time for new mentions that AI models haven't indexed yet.
- 🔥 **Amplify**: Maximize existing wins.
- 🆕 **Emerging**: Get ahead of new trends.
- ⚔️ **Competitive**: Respond to competitor victories.

## Automation & Scheduling

Save time by automating your monitoring workflows.

1. Navigate to the **Schedules** tab.
2. Create recurring audit runs (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
3. The system will automatically run audits, track trends, and notify you of changes.

## Reporting & Exports

Provide your clients with professional, data-driven reports with one click.

- **Full Audit Report (TXT)**: A comprehensive narrative report including executive summaries, competitor gaps, and actionable recommendations.
- **Data Exports (CSV)**: Raw data for use in your own internal spreadsheets or client BI tools.
- **Citation Intelligence Report**: Detailed analysis of the source landscape for outreach teams.

---

## Quotas & Limits

As an agency user, your account has specific resource allocations:

- **Brand Quota**: Up to 5 managed client brands.
- **Prompt Quota**: Up to 15 active prompts per brand.
- **Audit Tracking**: Unlimited audit history for all active clients.
